About

About CampusNav

CampusNav is an intelligent campus navigation system designed to help students, staff and visitors find important places around the institution.

About the School

Federal Polytechnic Ilaro provides technical, technological and professional education. The campus contains academic buildings, administrative offices, laboratories, lecture halls and other facilities.

Why CampusNav?

CampusNav helps users find unfamiliar locations faster and provides a centralized digital map for campus navigation.

Benefits

  • Easier campus location search.
  • Walking directions.
  • Voice search.
  • Google Maps place information.
  • Centralized campus navigation.

Developer

CampusNav was developed as a final-year Computer Science project applying modern mapping and navigation technologies to solve a practical campus problem.
